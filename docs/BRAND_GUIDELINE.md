# AETHER SENTINEL AI Brand Guideline

## Studio

AETHER AGENTIC Studio

## Creator

Anson

## Studio Vision

AETHER AGENTIC Studio focuses on building AI-native software agents that improve human productivity, creativity, and digital experiences.

## Product Name

AETHER SENTINEL AI

## Product Category

AI Performance Intelligence Agent

## Brand Meaning

### AETHER

AETHER represents:

- AI ecosystem.
- Future intelligence.
- High-level digital space.
- Invisible infrastructure that supports human creativity and productivity.

### SENTINEL

SENTINEL represents:

- Guardian.
- Protection.
- Monitoring.
- Awareness.
- Reliability.

## Brand Personality

AETHER SENTINEL AI should feel:

- Professional.
- Intelligent.
- Reliable.
- Minimal.
- Technical.
- Future-oriented.

Avoid:

- Cheap gaming RGB style.
- Cartoon gaming style.
- Excessive cyberpunk aesthetics.
- Loud effects that reduce trust.
- Visual clutter that makes performance data harder to understand.

## Visual Design Direction

References:

- Apple system design.
- NVIDIA professional tools.
- Linear.
- Notion.
- SpaceX engineering aesthetics.

Style attributes:

- Dark.
- Clean.
- Precise.
- AI-native.
- Calm.
- High signal.

## Color System

The color system must support dark professional interfaces, technical status feedback, and calm decision-making.

### Design Tokens

| Token | Role | Suggested Value | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `AetherDark` | Primary surface and brand anchor | `#0B0F14` | App shell, deep panels, navigation |
| `DeepSpace` | Background | `#05070A` | Main app background |
| `AIEnergyBlue` | Accent | `#2F80FF` | Primary actions, active states, AI signals |
| `PerformanceGreen` | Success | `#2BD576` | Healthy status, improvement, verified success |
| `OptimizationAmber` | Warning | `#F2B84B` | Risk, pending action, attention needed |
| `AlertRed` | Error | `#FF4D5E` | Failure, unsafe state, destructive risk |
| `TextPrimary` | Main text | `#F4F7FB` | Primary labels and headings |
| `TextSecondary` | Secondary text | `#A8B3C2` | Supporting copy and metadata |
| `TextMuted` | Muted text | `#6F7A89` | Disabled or low-priority information |
| `BorderSubtle` | Divider | `#1C2531` | Lines, separators, component borders |
| `SurfaceRaised` | Raised panel | `#101722` | Cards, dialogs, tool panels |

### Usage Rules

- Use `AIEnergyBlue` for primary action and AI emphasis only.
- Use status colors consistently and sparingly.
- Keep large surfaces neutral and dark.
- Do not let status colors dominate the interface.
- Avoid rainbow gradients and aggressive RGB lighting.

## Typography System

Platform fonts:

- Windows: Segoe UI.
- macOS: SF Pro.
- Fallback: Inter.

### Type Roles

| Role | Purpose | Weight | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Display | Product-level hero or score moments | Semibold | Use sparingly |
| Heading | Page and section titles | Semibold | Clear hierarchy |
| Title | Component titles | Medium | Compact and scannable |
| Body | Main interface text | Regular | Default reading style |
| Caption | Metadata and labels | Regular | Use with high contrast discipline |

## Voice And Tone

The product voice should be:

- Clear.
- Technical but understandable.
- Calm under risk.
- Direct about uncertainty.
- Helpful without exaggeration.

Avoid:

- Fake urgency.
- Overpromising performance improvements.
- Vague claims such as "boost everything instantly".
- Blaming the user.

## Product Promise

AETHER SENTINEL AI helps the user understand what is happening, why it matters, what can be improved, what risk exists, and whether the improvement worked.
